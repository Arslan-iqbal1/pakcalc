Fiverr and Upwork Fees: What Freelancers Actually Keep

A $100 order is not $100 in your pocket. Platforms charge a service fee, withdrawals cost more, and currency conversion takes another cut. Knowing the real number before you accept a project changes how you price your work.

Fiverr

Fiverr charges sellers a 20% service fee on every order, including tips. A $100 order leaves you $80 before withdrawal costs. Withdrawal options (Payoneer, bank transfer) have their own fees, and the PKR rate you receive is set by the withdrawal provider.

Upwork

Upwork's standard freelancer service fee is 10% on most contracts. So $100 of billed work leaves $90 before withdrawal fees. Enterprise or special contracts can differ, so check your own contract's fee tier.

Pricing lessons

Quote on your net, not your gross: decide the PKR amount you need, add back the platform fee, withdrawal fee and a buffer for rate movement, and that is your asking price. Track every fee in a simple sheet - over a year, the difference between guessing and knowing is often a full month's income.

Frequently asked questions

Does Fiverr charge 20% on tips too?

Yes, the 20% service fee applies to order amounts including tips.

Is Upwork's fee always 10%?

10% is the standard rate on most contracts; check your contract for exceptions.

Why is my bank amount lower than the calculator?

Banks and withdrawal providers apply their own exchange rate and may charge receiving fees.

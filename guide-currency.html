USD to PKR Exchange: A Practical Guide

The rupee-dollar rate moves every working day with trade, remittances, reserves and market sentiment. For freelancers, overseas Pakistanis and importers, a few rupees per dollar can mean thousands of rupees in a single payment.

Interbank vs open market vs bank rate

The interbank rate is the reference rate banks use with each other. The open-market rate is what money changers quote, usually slightly higher. Your bank's own rate for incoming payments can be lower than both, and some banks add conversion charges. Always confirm the rate your own bank will actually apply.

Getting a better rate

Avoid converting on volatile days if you can wait, compare two or three channels before large transfers, and check whether your platform (Fiverr, Upwork, Payoneer, Wise) converts at its own rate - that rate, not the Google rate, decides what you receive.

A note on forecasts

Nobody reliably predicts exchange rates short-term. Anyone guaranteeing a future rate is guessing. Plan budgets with a margin, and convert regular income steadily rather than gambling on one day's rate.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the Google rate different from my bank's rate?

Google shows the mid-market reference rate; banks and platforms add their own margin and fees.

Which rate should freelancers use for planning?

Use your platform's actual payout rate, or a conservative estimate slightly below the market rate.

Do rates change on weekends?

Formal markets are closed, but informal and crypto-linked rates can still move.

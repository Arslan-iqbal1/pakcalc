Pakistan Income Tax Slabs 2025-26 Explained

If you earn a salary in Pakistan, your employer deducts income tax every month under Section 149 of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001. The Finance Act 2025 changed the slabs for salaried individuals, giving relief to middle incomes. Here is how the system works.

The slabs (annual taxable salary)

Up to Rs 600,000: no tax. Rs 600,001-1,200,000: 1% of the amount above 600,000. Rs 1,200,001-2,200,000: Rs 6,000 plus 11% above 1,200,000. Rs 2,200,001-3,200,000: Rs 116,000 plus 23% above 2,200,000. Rs 3,200,001-4,100,000: Rs 346,000 plus 30% above 3,200,000. Above Rs 4,100,000: Rs 616,000 plus 35% above 4,100,000. Income above Rs 10 million also attracts a 9% surcharge on the tax.

A worked example

Monthly salary Rs 150,000 means annual salary Rs 1,800,000. That falls in the 1.2M-2.2M slab: Rs 6,000 + 11% of Rs 600,000 = Rs 72,000 per year, or Rs 6,000 per month. Your take-home is Rs 144,000 per month before other deductions.

Gross salary is not taxable salary

Tax applies to taxable income, not your full gross pay. Some allowances have exemptions, and the exact treatment depends on FBR rules. Our calculator works on the salary figure you enter, so enter your taxable salary for the most accurate result, and confirm with your payroll department or a tax consultant before filing.

Frequently asked questions

Who pays no income tax?

Salaried individuals earning up to Rs 600,000 a year (Rs 50,000 a month) pay zero tax.

Why does my employer deduct tax monthly?

Employers must estimate your annual tax, divide by 12, and deposit it with FBR each month.

Do these slabs apply to business income?

No. Business individuals and companies have separate slab structures.

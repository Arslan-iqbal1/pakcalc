Pakistan Income Tax Slabs 2025-26 Explained

If you earn a salary in Pakistan, your employer deducts income tax every month under Section 149 of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001. The Finance Act 2025 changed the slabs for salaried individuals, giving relief to middle incomes. Here is how the system works.

The slabs for FY 2026-27 (annual taxable salary, Finance Act 2026)

Up to Rs 600,000: no tax. Rs 600,001-1,200,000: 1% of the amount above 600,000. Rs 1,200,001-2,200,000: Rs 6,000 plus 11% above 1,200,000. Rs 2,200,001-3,200,000: Rs 116,000 plus 20% above 2,200,000. Rs 3,200,001-4,100,000: Rs 316,000 plus 25% above 3,200,000. Rs 4,100,001-5,600,000: Rs 541,000 plus 29% above 4,100,000. Rs 5,600,001-7,000,000: Rs 976,000 plus 32% above 5,600,000. Above Rs 7,000,000: Rs 1,424,000 plus 35% above 7,000,000. Note: the 9% surcharge on salaried income was abolished in the Finance Act 2026. The previous year (FY 2025-26) used higher middle rates (23% and 30%) and kept the surcharge - our calculator offers both years so you can compare.

Worked examples

Monthly salary Rs 150,000 means annual salary Rs 1,800,000. That falls in the 1.2M-2.2M slab: Rs 6,000 + 11% of Rs 600,000 = Rs 72,000 per year, or Rs 6,000 per month. Your take-home is Rs 144,000 per month before other deductions. Second example: Rs 250,000 a month is Rs 3,000,000 a year, in the 2.2M-3.2M slab: Rs 116,000 + 20% of Rs 800,000 = Rs 276,000 per year, or Rs 23,000 per month.

Gross salary is not taxable salary

Tax applies to taxable income, not your full gross pay. Some allowances have exemptions, and the exact treatment depends on FBR rules. Our calculator works on the salary figure you enter, so enter your taxable salary for the most accurate result, and confirm with your payroll department or a tax consultant before filing.

Frequently asked questions

Who pays no income tax?

Salaried individuals earning up to Rs 600,000 a year (Rs 50,000 a month) pay zero tax.

Did the slabs change from last year?

Yes. FY 2026-27 lowered the middle slabs (20% instead of 23%, 25% instead of 30%), added new 29% and 32% bands above Rs 4.1 million, and abolished the 9% surcharge on salaried income.

Why does my employer deduct tax monthly?

Employers must estimate your annual tax, divide by 12, and deposit it with FBR each month.

Do these slabs apply to business income?

No. Business individuals and companies have separate slab structures.

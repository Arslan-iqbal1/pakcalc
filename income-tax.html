Income Tax Calculator Pakistan (FY 2026-27)

Salaried individuals: enter your monthly salary and see your annual tax, monthly deduction and take-home pay under the Finance Act 2026 slabs. You can also switch to FY 2025-26 for comparison.

Your salary

FY 2026-27 slabs: 0% to Rs 600,000; 1% on 600k-1.2M; 11% on 1.2M-2.2M (Rs 6,000 + 11%); 20% on 2.2M-3.2M (Rs 116,000 + 20%); 25% on 3.2M-4.1M (Rs 316,000 + 25%); 29% on 4.1M-5.6M (Rs 541,000 + 29%); 32% on 5.6M-7M (Rs 976,000 + 32%); 35% above 7M (Rs 1,424,000 + 35%). The 9% surcharge on salaried income was abolished in the Finance Act 2026. Verify with FBR before filing.
